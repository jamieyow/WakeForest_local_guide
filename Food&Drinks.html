Food and Drinks

The food and drinks here are unique and something you won't soon forget!

Restaurants & Bars

Farm Table

A locally owned farm-to-table restaurant that has been part of Wake Forest since 2014. The menu is built around fresh, local ingredients, and there's a patio for warm evenings. It's a good choice for a sit-down dinner.

Address: 960 Gateway Commons Cir.

Best for: dinner, special occasions

Broadsides & Brews

A cozy downtown bar with a book-lover's theme that calls itself "Wake Forest's Living Room." It's known for creative, carefully made cocktails and a relaxed place to sit and talk.

Address: 223 S. White St.

Best for: evening drinks, meeting friends

Bodega Tapas & Wine

A small downtown restaurant serving tapas, which are small plates meant for sharing. The menu has hot and cold dishes with flavors from around the world, plus a strong wine list. Order a few plates and try a little of everything.

Address: 110 S. White St.

Best for: sharing with friends, wine lovers

Tips for Eating Downtown

  • Weekend evenings get busy, so plan to arrive early or make a reservation.
  • Bodega Tapas & Wine and Broadsides & Brews are both on White Street, so you can walk from dinner to drinks.
  • Farm Table is a short drive from downtown, so plan for parking there.
  • Hours change by season, so check a restaurant's hours before you go.
