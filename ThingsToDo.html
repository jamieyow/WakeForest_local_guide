Things to Do

On this page you will find things to do and places to visit.

Places to Visit

The Factory

A large sports and entertainment complex on South Main Street with something for every age. There's ice skating at Polar Ice, laser tag at Battle House, a big indoor and outdoor skatepark, and baseball and softball fields. Plan on two to three hours to try a few activities.

Address: 1839 S. Main St.

Best for: families, rainy days, groups

E. Carroll Joyner Park

The town's largest park covers more than 100 acres of rolling fields, walking trails, and gardens. Bring a picnic, take a walk, or come back in the summer for outdoor concerts and movie nights.

Address: 701 Harris Rd.

Cost: free

Strike & Barrel

A boutique bowling alley in historic downtown, with 8 classic lanes. Between games you can play bocce, cornhole, or arcade games, and the restaurant and full bar serve food and drinks.

Address: 413 S. Brooks St.

Best for: a night out, friends, birthday parties

Tips for Your Visit

  • Joyner Park is free, so it's an easy stop between other activities.
  • Strike & Barrel is downtown, a short walk from the restaurants on White Street.
  • The Factory has many different businesses, and each one sets its own hours.
  • Hours and prices change, so check before you go.
