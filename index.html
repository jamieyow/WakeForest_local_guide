Welcome to Wake Forest, North Carolina!

Wake Forest is a beautiful and fun town to visit, with many things for first-time visitors, families, and foodies to discover. In this local guide you will find places to eat, grab a drink, things to do, and places to see. Wake Forest became a town in 1880. This town may have started small, but it has grown into a bustling and vibrant community. Wake Forest is unique because there is so much this town has to offer.

A Perfect Day in Wake Forest

Not sure where to start? Try this plan for a full day in town.

  1. Morning: start at The Factory for ice skating, laser tag, or the skatepark.
  2. Midday: pack a picnic and walk the trails at E. Carroll Joyner Park.
  3. Afternoon: head downtown and bowl a few games at Strike & Barrel.
  4. Dinner: enjoy a farm-to-table meal at Farm Table, or share small plates at Bodega Tapas & Wine.
  5. Evening: end the day with a cocktail at Broadsides & Brews.

Getting Here

From Raleigh, take US-1 North (Capital Boulevard) and follow the signs for downtown Wake Forest. Free street parking and public lots are available near White Street. Once you're downtown, the easiest way to get around is on foot.

For events, maps, and news, visit the official Town of Wake Forest website.
